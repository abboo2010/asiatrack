Our Story

Built to meet the demands of Malaysia’s water and process industries

Asia Track (M) Sdn. Bhd. was established with a clear objective: to provide dependable pipeline and tank solutions that meet the growing demands of Malaysia’s water and process industries. From our early beginnings, we have steadily grown into a trusted industry partner, recognised for quality fabrication, disciplined execution and professional service delivery.

As industry requirements evolved, so did our capabilities. Today, we offer end-to-end pipe fabrication, including conventional steel systems and advanced materials such as GRP and HDPE, alongside tank installation, maintenance, repair services, and the supply of sectional panel and FRP/GRP tanks.

Our journey is built on technical expertise, continuous improvement, and a commitment to delivering systems that are safe, efficient, and engineered to last.

Pipe Fabrication

End-to-end pipeline fabrication

We provide end-to-end pipeline fabrication solutions for a wide range of industries, delivering durable, precise and compliant piping systems tailored to your project requirements.

Our fabrication process includes cutting, welding, jointing, surface treatment and quality inspection to ensure strength, accuracy and long service life.

GRP pipe fabrication

We also specialise in GRP (Glass Reinforced Plastic) pipe fabrication: lightweight yet high-strength piping, highly resistant to corrosion and chemicals, ideal for water supply, sewage, industrial and marine applications, especially in aggressive environments.

  • Skilled fabrication team with industry experience
  • Compliance with project specifications and standards
  • High-quality workmanship and strict quality control
  • Suitable for industrial, commercial and infrastructure projects

Conventional pipe fabrication

We fabricate and assemble pipelines using commonly specified materials:

  • MSMild Steel — cost-effective and widely used for general piping applications
  • SSStainless Steel — corrosion-resistant, suitable for hygienic and high-performance systems
  • MSCLMild Steel Cement Lined — ideal for water transmission and distribution
  • HDGHot-Dip Galvanized — enhanced corrosion protection for outdoor and exposed environments
  • HDPEHigh-Density Polyethylene — lightweight, flexible, resistant to chemicals and corrosion
